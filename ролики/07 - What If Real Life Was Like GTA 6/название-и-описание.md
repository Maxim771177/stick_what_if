What If Your Life Was Like GTA 6?

## Описание
Monday, 8:10 AM, room 114. A yellow circle glows beside Leon's desk, and above it floats a white line: Mission. Pass the math test. Reward, $20,000. Nobody else can see it. So what if your life was like GTA 6?

Here are the four rules: every task in your day is a mission that pays real money; whatever you buy shows up in your garage in seconds; break the rules and stars appear in the corner of your eye, but you can always lose them; and a health bar floats over your head, so if it hits zero you wake up in a hospital, fully healed, a few minutes later.

Then comes the fun part: a million dollars in five days (a typical full-time worker in the US would need about seventeen years of paychecks, based on BLS median weekly earnings), a hypercar with a bow on the roof, a speedboat, a red helicopter, a police chase through a car wash, a jump from the school roof, and the first mission in Leon's life that pays nothing.

We also look at the real number behind the scale: in 2019 a modified Bugatti Chiron reached 304 mph on a German test track.
