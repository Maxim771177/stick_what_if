## Описание
You're the smartest kid in the room by a margin nobody has ever measured. Sounds perfect, right? Then the problems start.

What if you had an IQ of 9,999? In this video, Leon turns the page before the teacher says start, finishes a 50-question test in seconds... and then finds out what a mind that fast actually costs: a brain that burns a fifth of your body's energy, a body that can't keep up, a night without sleep, a friend who walks away, and one frozen pond that tests everything.

We cover: why real IQ tests stop around 160, why IQ is a rank and not a pile of points, how fast a nerve signal really travels (about 400 km/h), why the brain needs sleep to clean itself (a 2013 study in Science, on mice), and what psychologist Leta Hollingworth found in 1942 about children with very high IQ.
