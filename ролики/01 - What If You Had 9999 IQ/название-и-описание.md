## Название
What If You Had an IQ of 9,999?

## Описание
What if you were the smartest kid in the room, by a thousand times?

Meet Leon. His IQ is 9,999 and everyone around him is still stuck at 60, 80 and 100. Sounds like the perfect life, right? Then the problems start: a brain that burns a fifth of your energy, a body that can't keep up, no sleep, no friends on your level, and one frozen pond that tests everything.

We break down what IQ actually measures, why real tests stop near 160, and what the science says about hunger, sleep, reaction speed and loneliness for a very fast mind.

Part 2 is coming. Subscribe so you don't miss it, and tell us in the comments: what would you do with a mind like that?
