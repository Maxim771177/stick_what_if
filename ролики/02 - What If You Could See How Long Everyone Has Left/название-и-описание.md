What If You Could See How Long Everyone Has Left to Live?

## Описание
You can see a health bar floating above every head, with the exact time that person has left. Sounds like a superpower. It is, until the bar over your own family turns pink.

What if you could see how long everyone has left to live? In this video, Leon walks through his school hallway with a bar above every head, learns the rules, enjoys the perks... and then meets the problems: a crowd of thousands of bars, a best friend who wants proof, and one Saturday morning in the kitchen when Mom, Dad, Nora and the dog all turn pink at once.

We cover: why an average life span (about 73 years worldwide, according to the WHO) is not the same as your own bar, how many moving things a person can track at once (about four, Pylyshyn and Storm, 1988), why doctors guessed five times too long in a study of 468 patients (Christakis and Lamont, BMJ, 2000), and why carbon monoxide, which sticks to hemoglobin about 200 to 250 times harder than oxygen, is so easy to mistake for a bug (the CDC counted about 430 deaths a year in 1999 to 2010, not counting fires).
