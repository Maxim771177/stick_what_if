## Название (45 симв.)
What If Your Punch Hit With 3 Tons of Force?

## Описание
Saturday afternoon, the spring fair. Owen hits the strength tester for 310. A dad in a bandana hits 640 and wins a plush shark. Then Leon walks up, ninety pounds and a backwards cap, and throws one punch. The bag does not swing. It leaves. It clears the lemonade stand, slams into the dunk tank, and the screen flashes E, R, R. So what if your punch hit with three tons of force, and nothing around you is built for it?

The rules are simple: it only works when you throw a punch, a full swing is six thousand pounds, and the force follows your effort. Your body stays exactly the same size. That last part is going to matter.

Along the way: a tent stake that disappears, a locker door that comes off like a pizza box lid, Isaac Newton and the recoil that sends Leon into a hedge, a carton of eggs and twelve omelets, and a steel door that dents a sore hand but not the door. Then a hot Saturday, a steel container, a little girl called Poppy, and exactly one chance to get it right.
