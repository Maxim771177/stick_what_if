## Описание (вставить как есть)
Your boat hits a reef. A coach rows off for help. And you are the only boy stuck on an island with 19 girls from the school sailing team, a bag of glow sticks, and 72 hours until anyone comes back.

Can you survive three days when the girls' captain has already decided you are dead weight? In this video Leon, our stickman with the backwards cap, goes through the whole story step by step: the Rule of Threes, why you can't drink seawater, how to read a tide line, what a rip current really does to a raft, and how a bag of glow sticks can save everyone.
