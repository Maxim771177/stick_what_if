What If You Could See 10 Seconds Into the Future?

## Описание
Tuesday, 12:05, the school cafeteria. Leon is holding a tray of tomato soup when he just stops. His eyes go glassy, and three seconds later he catches Owen's tray at the edge of the table without looking. So what if you could see exactly ten seconds into the future?

The rules look like a gift: you see ten seconds ahead, you change one thing, and the whole movie updates. The catch is rule four. Every glimpse freezes you for about three seconds, and those seconds come out of the ten.

Along the way: a penalty challenge for ten thousand dollars (a hard shot from eleven meters reaches the net in about half a second), a scooter on the way home, a school day where nobody can hit you in dodgeball, and a famous 1983 experiment by Benjamin Libet that asks whether your brain decides before you do. Then comes a winter concert, a steel frame full of spotlights, and a choice with seven seconds on the clock.
