What If You Had Infinite IQ and Had to Hide It?

## Описание
Monday, lunch, the school cafeteria. A Rubik's Cube sits on a shelf, scrambled so badly that the cube club has stared at it for a whole semester. Leon solved it before he sat down. He just can't touch it, not yet. So what if you had an infinite IQ and had to hide it?

The rules are simple: never sign anything, never let anyone watch you solve it, and solve it ugly. The problem is that your brain has no ugly mode. By Friday, an inspector with a clipboard will be hunting for the mystery solver the school newspaper calls the Ghost.

Along the way: the superflip, the hardest Rubik's Cube position (every cube can be solved in twenty moves or fewer, proven in 2010 with about thirty-five years of computer time), a sheet of paper folded twelve times (Britney Gallivan, 2002, about 1.2 km of paper), a traffic jam of four hundred cars fixed with a napkin and a "green wave", and a locked door in a clock tower that nobody has opened since 1989.
