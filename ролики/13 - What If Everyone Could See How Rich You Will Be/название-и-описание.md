## Описание (вставить как есть)
One morning a gold number lights up above Leon's head: $27,000,000,000. The whole school can read it. Girls hand him cookies, a stranger in a gray suit offers him a contract, and every choice he makes moves the number up or down.

In this video Leon, our stickman with the backwards cap, follows the number step by step, from Tuesday's homeroom to Friday's science fair. You will see why a number can make people nice to you for the wrong reason, what the halo effect is, why winning big money does not make everyday moments better, and what the number was really counting all along.

Real facts used in the video: the halo effect (Edward Thorndike, 1920), the lottery-winner study by Brickman, Coates and Janoff-Bulman (1978), and the numbers behind pedal power (a person can keep up about 100 watts on a bicycle, a phone charges on about 5).
