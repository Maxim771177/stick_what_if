## Название (36 симв.)
What If You Could See Everyone's IQ?

## Описание
Tuesday, second period. Ms. Park hands back the math tests: a giant red A-plus for Caleb, a giant red F for Leon. Then a number appears above Caleb's head. Seventy. In the window, Leon sees his own reflection. One hundred and sixty. So what if you could see everyone's IQ, and the numbers and the grades refuse to agree?

The rules sound like a gift: the number is real, and only you can see it. The catch is rule three. It measures how fast a brain handles brand-new puzzles. It does not measure effort, and it does not measure homework. Leon, who has never read a manual in his life, skips that part.

Along the way: a bus driver, a man fighting a paper map, a pizza maker who scores eighty-seven and makes the best pizza in town, a library at six-forty in the morning, a thick green notebook full of mistakes, a 2005 study of one hundred and forty eighth graders, and a famous 1921 study of gifted children where two rejected kids went on to win Nobel Prizes. Then comes a twenty-step Rube Goldberg machine, forty-one failed runs, and a gym door that nobody tested.
