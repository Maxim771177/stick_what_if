## Название (45 симв., утверждено владельцем 13.11.2026)
What If You Doubled in Size Every 10 Minutes?

## Описание
Leon is ten years old and four and a half feet tall. Then the bus line starts, and every ten minutes he doubles in size. At 3:10 he is nine feet tall. By 4:00 he is taller than a 28-story building. By 6:00 he is standing over the Earth, and his little sister's eighth birthday cake is still 223 miles below him.

What if you doubled in size every 10 minutes? In this video Leon walks over a traffic jam, dunks a basketball without jumping, makes the school bully give back the desserts... and then runs into the real problems: weight, bones, slow nerves, thin air, and the silence of space.

We cover: why every doubling makes you 8 times heavier but your bones only 4 times stronger (Galileo, Two New Sciences, 1638), why a giant's nerve signal, travelling at up to about 120 meters per second, would take about 14 seconds to get from his foot to his brain, how little air is left at the top of Mount Everest (29,032 feet, about a third of the pressure at sea level), why the edge of space is drawn 62 miles up (the Karman line), and why sound cannot cross a vacuum, so Leon's mom, his sister and the whole town would go silent.
